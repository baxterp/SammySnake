About me

Built on speed, patience and a lot of laps

I am Sammy, the snake behind Sammysnake Racing. I race Gran Turismo 7 almost every day, take on competitive online lobbies, and spend a lot of time figuring out where the seconds really come from.

The channel is for drivers who want to get faster without guesswork. Every video is about the line, the brake point and the mindset that keeps you consistent when the pack gets tight.

Recommended equipment

The rig that keeps Sammy fast

The core setup for sim racing at home. Start with the wheel and pedals, then build out.

  • Direct-drive steering wheel

    Strong force feedback and fast response so you can feel the car load up through the corners.

    Priority one

  • Load-cell pedal set

    Pressure-based braking builds muscle memory that transfers to every track.

    Priority one

  • Wheel-deck or racing seat

    A rigid, consistent mounting position keeps your inputs repeatable lap after lap.

    Priority two

  • Sequential shifter

    Quick, precise gear changes and a handbrake when you want to rotate the car.

    Priority two

  • Large display

    A bigger screen gives you more peripheral vision and earlier reads on the apex.
